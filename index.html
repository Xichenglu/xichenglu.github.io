Xicheng Lu

Contact: xicheng.lu@qmul.ac.uk

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London, U.K., supervised by Prof. Akram Alomainy (QMUL) and Prof. Wei Liu (PolyU). I received the B.Sc. degree in electrical engineering from Fuzhou University, China, in 2020, and the M.Sc. degree in electronic and electrical engineering from the University of Sheffield, U.K., in 2022. My research interests include low-bit quantization, phase retrieval, and array signal processing.
